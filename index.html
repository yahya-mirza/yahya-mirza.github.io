Mechanical Engineering Student @ UC San Diego | Robotics & Mechatronics

Hey I'm Yahya, nice to meet you! At this website, you can find my engineering portfolio and resume below.

In a rush? Take a look at my work over at Yonder Dynamics as well as take a look at my whiteboard robot!

At Yonder Dynamics, UCSD's competition mars rover team, I successfully led an end-to-end production of a linear slider mechanism capable of laterally translating a 30lb robotic arm. In my free time, I have successfully built an x-y gantry system for a whiteboard drawing robot, a soccer robot equipped with a 9:1 flywheel kicker system (with the honor of "Best Manufacturing"), and co-founded/lead Foothill College's award-winning FireFlight RC Plane, an ML-Driven, aerial sensing RC Plane for wildfire prediction and evacuation.
